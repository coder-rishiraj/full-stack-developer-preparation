Pay bands and distributions

Statistics

This group matters more in HR than people expect, because pay is always described as a distribution. A band is a minimum, a midpoint and a maximum; a market rate is a percentile; a person's position in the band is a ratio. Knowing the difference between a mean and a median in a salary conversation is a credibility question.

Why the median, not the average, for salary. One very senior person pulls the average up and makes everyone else look underpaid relative to it. The median is the middle person, and it does not move when an outlier is added. If someone quotes an average salary, the first question is what the median is.

Compa-ratio is salary ÷ band midpoint. 1.0 means paid at the midpoint, 0.85 means well below it. It is the number a compensation partner will ask your for, and it is one division.

Reading the error, not panicking at it

The eight errors and what each one means

ErrorIt meansDo this
#DIV/0!Divided by zero or by an empty cell.Expected early in a month when there are no offers yet. Wrap it: =IFERROR(a/b,"").
#N/AA lookup found nothing.Check the key for a trailing space or a text-vs-number mismatch. Use XLOOKUP's fourth argument to label it instead of hiding it.
#VALUE!Wrong kind of thing — arithmetic on text.Usually a number stored as text. Test it with ISNUMBER.
#REF!The formula points at a cell that no longer exists.Something was deleted. Undo if it just happened; otherwise rebuild the reference.
#NAME?Excel does not recognise a word in the formula.A misspelt function name, a missing quote, or a table name that does not exist.
#NUM!A number that is impossible.A square root of a negative, or a percentile outside 0–1.
#SPILL!A dynamic array has nowhere to land.Clear the cells below. Do not shorten the formula.
#CALC!An array operation produced nothing.Usually a FILTER that matched no rows — give it a third argument: FILTER(a,b,"None").

The rule about IFERROR. It hides every error, not the one expected. Wrapping a whole dashboard in IFERROR means a genuinely broken formula looks like a blank cell and nobody finds out for a month. Wrap the single division that can legitimately divide by zero, and leave the rest visible.
