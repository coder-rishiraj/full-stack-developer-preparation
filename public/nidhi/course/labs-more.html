Measures, not columns

Power BI: write the measures again

A calculated column is a fact about one row (days to hire). A measure is a question that must change when a slicer is clicked (average days, acceptance rate). DIVIDE rather than / so a month with no offers is blank, not an error. CALCULATE replaces a filter on the same column; it does not stack a second one. ALL in a denominator is how a percent-of-total stays a percent of the grand total.

On this practice file: six applicants, four offers, three accepts, two joiners, €4,000 of fees. Acceptance is DIVIDE of accepts over offers = 0.75. Write the measures. Then answer the quiz as if a hiring manager is watching the slicer.

Interview line. “Days to hire is a column. Average days to hire is a measure. If it must move when someone clicks Dublin, it is a measure.”

Two clocks, two stories

Time to fill is not time to hire

Time to hire is the candidate’s wait: accepted (or offer) minus applied. Time to fill is the seat’s wait: the person starts minus the requisition opened. They disagree on purpose. Dev applied in February to a role opened in January — his hire-days are 32, the seat waited longer. Asha is the clean 23. Quote both, and say which question the manager asked.

Joined minus Accepted is notice, not time to hire. Asha: 20 Apr − 25 Mar = 26 days. Eva has no join date, so notice is blank. Mixing notice into time to hire is how a dashboard blames the recruiter for Irish statutory notice.

Interview line. “Time to hire is the process. Time to fill is the vacancy. Notice sits in between and it is not my speed.”

The fee that survived a renege

Cost per hire, honestly

Internal cost is hours × loaded rate. Agency cost is the fee, owed even when Eva never starts. Cost per hire = total cost / joiners, not / offers. Two joiners here. If you divide by four offers you are lying about productivity.

Volume is not yield

Source of hire vs source of applicants

LinkedIn is four of six applicants and both joiners. Referral is one applicant and one joiner (100% conversion, sample size one). Agency is one applicant, one accept, zero joiners, €4,000. Source of hire without conversion is a vanity chart.

A stage is not a person

ATS: why funnel reports lie

A requisition is the job. An application is a person on a job. An offer is a later object. If you count “interviewed” from a stage history that people can skip, or from events rather than people, the funnel will not add up. Disposition is why someone left the process — “not a fit” is lazy; “missing CNC 5-axis, three years” is usable. Do not write Workday as professional experience unless you have used it at work.

Bracket every OR

Boolean that returns the right people

AND narrows. OR widens and must sit in brackets. NOT loses good people — use it for job-seeker noise (“recruiter”, “hiring”), not for skills you are unsure about. Quotes for a phrase. Build the string, then read who it kept.

False matches cost weeks

Roles: Java is not JavaScript

Frontend paints the screen (React, CSS). Backend serves data (Java, Python, Go, APIs). Full stack is both, and often neither at the depth the manager wanted. A data analyst is not a data engineer. ML engineer is not “someone who used ChatGPT”. Your manufacturing ground (CNC, CAM, CMM) is a strength for industrial TA roles — it is not a substitute for knowing what a backend engineer does in Dublin.

The numbers you must not mix up

Visas, notice, GDPR

Ireland Critical Skills listed occupations from €40,904; other eligible roles from €68,911. Netherlands highly skilled migrant 30+ is €5,942 a month excluding holiday allowance, recognised sponsor. Germany EU Blue Card 2026 standard €50,700. GDPR: purpose, minimisation, retention, access, erasure. Candidate data is not practice data. US employment law from a Coursera course does not transfer.

Reset the brief before sourcing

Intake, workforce, the hard conversation

A partner looks at the market, resets expectations, chooses channels, sizes the funnel, watches conversion. An order-taker takes a JD and posts it. If sourced-per-offer is huge and offers are zero, that is a spec conversation on Monday, not more job boards.

Helpful, then it stops

AI: what you will and will not paste

Allowed on invented or already-public text: Boolean drafts, JD structure, interview-question lists, a market note. Never: a real CV, phone, pay, interview notes, or anything that could identify a candidate. If the tool invents a skill, you have to catch it — that is the job. IBM’s Generative AI for HR comes after your own files exist.

Interview: story, salary, the gap

Salary for a TA Partner in Ireland: €70,000–€80,000 gross, sponsorable. Never open at €50,000.
